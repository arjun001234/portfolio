What I do

Platform engineering, DevOps and SRE

Three overlapping jobs, one goal: let product teams ship fast on infrastructure that doesn't fall over.

Platform engineer

I build internal developer platforms: Backstage self-service templates, GitOps with Argo CD and Kargo, and multi-tenant Amazon EKS with observability built in, so teams onboard and deploy without raising infrastructure tickets.

DevOps engineer

I automate the path from commit to production: GitLab CI and Jenkins pipelines, Helm, Terraform modules with remote state and drift detection, and AWS infrastructure as code, including agentic AI that helps create and maintain it.

Site reliability engineer

I keep systems up under real load: SLOs and alerting, k6 load testing, Karpenter and KEDA autoscaling, multi-region disaster recovery with backup-restore and failover drills, and on-call for events like the FIFA World Cup 2026.
